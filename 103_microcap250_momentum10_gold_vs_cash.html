▲ how this universe was built — and a data bug caught before publication

This is the first universe in the series that was not already cached. The constituent list came from the index provider's official CSV (fetched 2026-10-18): 249 Series=EQ names, of which 246 had price history through our data source. The 3 without any were kept out of the universe rather than guessed at. The eligibility floor (40 of 249 with a full 12m+6m history) never binds — 74 names already qualify at the 2007 start.

Disclosure worth making: the first bulk download silently returned foreign price series mixed into 113 of the 246 tickers — no error was raised, so no retry fired. It was caught by cross-checking against this project's earlier price caches before this report ran. The cache was then rebuilt one symbol at a time and gated against all 18 tickers it shares with those earlier caches: spans match, prices deviate 0.0000%. Every number below comes from that verified cache, not the corrupt one.

Why the filter helps least on this universe

▲ mechanism

WHAT THIS SHOWS — the mechanism behind the smallest drawdown cut in the series.

The filter spends 24.3% of trading days risk-off (the same 69 regime re-entries as every other universe in this series — the gate is driven by NIFTY 50 itself, not the holdings). During that time the cash variant earns nothing, so its CAGR sits 0.9 points below doing nothing (+42.5% vs. +43.4%) — the only universe where plain cash clearly costs return. Gold's +13.1% sleeve is what turns risk-off time into a net positive, lifting the hedged variant 3.1 points above the unfiltered strategy.

The drawdown cut is thin for a structural reason: microcap losses are largely idiosyncratic — promoter problems, liquidity traps, circuit-to-circuit collapses — and those strikes happen while NIFTY 50 is comfortably above its 200-day EMA, so the gate stays risk-on and sails through them unhedged. The regime filter can only see the market tide, not the individual rips. That is also the honest limit of the five-for-five pattern: it is five universes on ONE historical path (this window contains 2008-09 once), not a guarantee gold always helps — gold can fall alongside equities in a genuine liquidity crunch.

Limitations

▲ read before trusting any number above

WHAT THIS SHOWS — every simplification behind this backtest.

  • Survivorship bias is strongest on this page of the whole project. Today's Microcap 250 membership is applied retroactively to 2008, but the index itself only launched in December 2024 — the pre-2025 "history" is a backfilled construct of today's constituents. Microcap failures and delistings are concentrated in exactly the names this list can no longer contain, which flatters every variant's absolute CAGR: treat the levels as not investable expectations. The RANKING (gold beats cash beats nothing on both axes) is the more robust finding — and it likely understates the true drawdown cuts too, since dead names cluster in crashes.
  • 246 of 249 constituents have prices (3 had none through our data source and were kept out, not guessed at); today's constituent list also carries names added later and drops names removed earlier.
  • Thin liquidity: microcap closes are noisier than any other universe here (multi-day halts, wide spreads, stale prints), interior halt gaps are forward-filled by the loader, and equal-rupee sizing ignores that halted names can't actually be traded.
  • Zero transaction costs on ANY regime switch (into or out of gold) — same disclosed omission as reports 42-49; microcap exits through circuit limits would realistically slip worse than anything modeled elsewhere.
  • GOLDBEES.NS's real history starts 2009-01-02 — the first two calendar days of this window use a forward/backward-filled gold price rather than a real traded one, negligible over an 18-year span.
  • Only ONE EMA span (200 days) and immediate-switch (no confirmation delay) were tested here; equal weighting, no F&O-eligibility screen, unadjusted prices, no dividends modeled — same as every other reconstruction in this project.
