This applies the IDENTICAL filter mechanics to report 12's NIFTY100 Momentum 10 (100-stock universe) — the same universe where front-loaded momentum weighting (report 41) was the ONE variant that actively backfired. Does this filter also break down on the narrowest universe, or is it a different kind of change entirely?

Unlike report 41's front-loaded-momentum test — where NIFTY100 was the ONE universe that broke the pattern seen elsewhere — this regime filter helps NIFTY100 about as much as it helps every other universe in reports 42-44. The two formula changes are simply different in kind: front-loading changes WHICH stocks get picked (and a narrow universe has fewer alternatives to fall back on when that goes wrong), while this filter changes WHEN the strategy is in the market at all, using a signal that has nothing to do with universe breadth.

Why this filter doesn't break down on NIFTY100 (unlike front-loading)

▲ framing

WHAT THIS SHOWS — the mechanism, not just the scoreboard.

Report 41 found front-loaded momentum weighting actively HURT NIFTY100 — a narrower universe gave the reformulated stock-picking formula fewer genuine alternatives to fall back on, so short-term spikes that later reversed were more likely to occupy a top-10 slot. This regime filter is a completely different kind of change: it doesn't touch stock SELECTION at all — it only decides whether to hold the SAME top-10 selection the unfiltered strategy would have picked anyway, or hold nothing. Since the signal (NIFTY 50's own 200-EMA) is entirely independent of which universe sits underneath it, there's no "fewer candidates to choose from" failure mode here — the filter works essentially the same way regardless of universe breadth, which is exactly the 23.7%-time-in-cash / 69-re-entries figures being IDENTICAL across reports 42-45.

The whipsaw cost from report 42 still applies unchanged: 69 re-entries against 36 scheduled rebalances, all free of charge in this model. That caveat doesn't depend on universe either — it would apply just as much here as on Midcap150.

Limitations

▲ read before trusting any number above

WHAT THIS SHOWS — every simplification behind this backtest.

  • Zero transaction costs on ANY regime switch — 69 re-entries over 18 years, all free in this model.
  • Cash earns exactly 0% while the filter is out of the market, same convention as reports 03, 42-44.
  • The regime signal and the strategy's own execution both act on the SAME day's close — no next-day execution lag modeled.
  • Only ONE EMA span (200 trading days) is tested here — see report 46 for a span-sensitivity comparison.
  • Today's fixed NIFTY100 constituent list (report 12's config) is applied retroactively across the whole window (survivorship bias).
  • Equal weighting, no F&O-eligibility screen, unadjusted prices, no dividends modeled — same as every other reconstruction here. This is a single, fixed 18-year historical path.
