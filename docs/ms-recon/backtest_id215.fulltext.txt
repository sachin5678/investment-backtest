Is this result the rules, or luck?
The robustness check runs the same rules two more ways:
Date luck: on every other rebalance day (days 15 to 25 of the month). The day you picked should not be the only one that works.
Both halves: on the first and the second half of the period separately. A sound rule should beat the benchmark in both, not only in one lucky stretch.

How it is worked out
Long term = held more than 12 months. Gains are after brokerage and costs.
Short-term losses offset short- and long-term gains; long-term losses only long-term gains; unused losses carry forward 8 years.
Holdings bought before Feb 2018 use the 31 Jan 2018 value as cost (grandfathering), where that price is in the data.
Each year's tax is taken as paid on 31 March from the portfolio.
Only listed equity shares and equity-oriented funds with STT paid get these rates. Gold, international and debt ETFs are taxed differently but are treated here like equity.
Not covered: surcharge, your other income and losses, and the relief when your other income is below the basic exemption limit.
