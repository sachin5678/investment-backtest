Sell without a T-PIN every time: enable DDPI
Without DDPI, your broker can take shares from your demat account only after you authorise them with your CDSL T-PIN, and a sell is rejected until you do. With DDPI (Demat Debit and Pledge Instruction) active, sells from Place orders go straight through. It is a one-time online form in your broker's app or website (look for DDPI in your profile or account settings; some brokers charge a small fee). Once it is active, mark it in the helper (DDPI next to the account) so we stop asking. Without DDPI: on Dhan the helper can send the T-PIN and open CDSL for you before selling; for other brokers, authorise in their app first.
