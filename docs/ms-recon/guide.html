How MomentumScore picks what to hold

Five simple steps, the same on every review day. You choose the rules; MomentumScore applies them to every stock, every time, without emotion.

Step 2 · Conditions

The gate: yes or no

A condition is a simple test. Stocks that fail are never ranked, and one you hold that stops passing is sold at the next review. Conditions are optional.

Only rising: 6-month return above 0%
Skip wild swings: volatility under 45%
Enough liquidity: ₹5 crore traded a day
Price range: e.g. at least ₹50

Under the hood

The five things a style looks at

Every style is a mix of these plain measures, each over a period from 1 week to 1 year.

MomentumHow much the price rose over the periodHigher is better
Risk-adjustedThe rise divided by how bumpy it wasSmooth rises win
Low volatilityHow calmly the price moves day to dayCalmer is better
SteadinessStill rising over both 6 and 12 monthsLasting rises win
LiquidityRupees traded a day, on average (stocks)Easier to trade

For strict rules

Funnel: narrow it down in rounds

Each round keeps only the best part of what is left. What survives every round is sorted, and the top is bought.

→ the top 20 of the sorted list are bought. The builder shows these counts live as you set the rounds.
