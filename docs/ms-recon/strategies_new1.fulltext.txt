Swings = how much the price jumps around (volatility, a year's worth). Daily turnover = average value traded a day over 6 months. Price vs EMA/SMA = how far the close is above (+) or below (−) that average, in %. Supertrend = (10, 3) on daily candles. From 52-week high = 0% at the high, −20% means 20% below it. From all-time high = the same against the highest price since 2012 (or since listing). India VIX = the market's expected volatility (from mid-2014; earlier dates pass), used only before a buy. Index P/E = the P/E NSE publishes for the index a line tracks; lines without one (gold, silver, liquid, foreign) are not held back by it.

Each one gets its own % of the freed cash, e.g. GOLDBEES 50% + SILVERBEES 25%: the other 25% stays cash. Up to 4. Any NSE symbol we have prices for: an ETF (any BeES, LIQUIDCASE...) or a stock. One listed later than a date in the backtest (e.g. SILVERBEES from Feb 2022) leaves its share in cash until then. Liquid ETFs that pay their return as extra units (LIQUIDBEES and similar, price stays near ₹1,000) show 0% in a backtest; growth-type liquid ETFs show the return in the price.
The trend is read from a broad index, with SUPERTREND or a moving average (for example the 200-day average of NIFTY 500). Optionally, park part of the freed cash in gold (GOLDBEES) until the trend turns up again.
Volatility filter: treat a jumpy market (India VIX high) as a down market too
India VIX above
Above it the rules do what the action above says (go cash or half, and park in gold if set), the same as a down-trend; below it they invest again. Works with or without the trend filter. India VIX data starts mid-2014; before that this filter stays out of the way.
Benchmark · for comparison only
The index your results are compared with: the dashed line on the chart, benchmark CAGR and the year-by-year table. It never changes the trades. It can differ from the trend index, e.g. trend on NIFTY 500 and benchmark NIFTY SMALLCAP 250 for a smallcap strategy.
