Why G/H work

They stack a second trend filter on the gold sleeve itself: hold gold during equity risk-off only while gold is in its own uptrend, else sit flat. This dodges gold's own crashes (2013, 2021–22) which often coincide with equity risk-off windows. Standalone check: gold filtered by its own 200-EMA returns 18.7% CAGR vs 13.2% buy-and-hold over the same window.

Caveats: same-day-close signal convention as every other report here, no transaction costs on gold↔cash switches, ~1240 usable gold days. Worth a full audited numbered report before trusting the 53% headline.
