Why G/H help (and by how little)

They stack a second trend filter on the gold sleeve itself: hold gold during equity risk-off only while gold is in its own uptrend, else sit flat — dodging gold's own crashes (2013, 2021–22) when they coincide with equity risk-off. Corrected, that refinement is worth +1.2pp (G) / +0.9pp (H) of CAGR over report 48, with max drawdowns 0.8pp / 0.2pp shallower. The published 53.2%/53.5% made roughly +10pp of the headline unliveable — the same artifact class as reports 91/92 and finding f10; the full audit is report 99. (A standalone gold-filtered-by-its-own-200-EMA-vs-buy-and-hold number was claimed here in an earlier draft but didn't trace to any computed result — removed rather than left unverified. Report f10's gold-strength guard, built on the real report-48 event loop, is the properly audited version of this idea.)

Caveats: every row on this page shares the 2026-10-17 data window and the prior-close convention (corrected — see report 99); no transaction costs on gold↔cash switches, ~1240 usable gold days. The corrected G/H margins of +0.9–1.2pp of CAGR over report 48 are cost-sensitive, and a variant to audit — not a numbered report.
